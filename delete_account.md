# Delete your BioAce account

**Last updated: July 30, 2026**

This page explains how to delete your **BioAce** account and what happens when you do. It exists so
you can request deletion even if you no longer have the app installed, per Google Play's account
deletion requirements. For how we handle your data more generally, see our
[Privacy Policy](https://namache.github.io/BioAce-Privacy-Policy/).

---

## Option 1 — Delete in the app (instant)

1. Open BioAce and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings → Privacy → Delete account**.
3. Confirm. Your account and its data are deleted immediately — there is no waiting period.

## Option 2 — Request deletion by email (no app required)

If you don't have the app installed, email **ace.education.technologies@gmail.com** from the same
address you used to sign in with Google, with the subject line **"Delete my BioAce account."** We
use that address to confirm the request matches your account, then delete it.

---

## What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

- Your profile — username, avatar, and email/name from Google Sign-In
- Your learning and gamification data — lesson progress, XP, streaks, ranks, achievements, weekly
  quests, chest claims, and unlocked avatars
- Your friend connections and friend requests
- Any feedback messages you submitted through the app
- Your Google-linked sign-in itself, so a signed-out device can no longer resume the account

This is **permanent and cannot be undone**. There is no recovery window or "undo" — deleting again
with the same Google account creates a brand-new, empty profile.

## What isn't deleted

- **Crash reports.** Firebase Crashlytics logs are tied to a random per-device installation ID, not
  to your account, so they aren't reachable by an account-deletion request and aren't linked back to
  you once your account is gone.
- **Legally required records**, if any apply, kept only as long as the law requires — see Section 8
  of our [Privacy Policy](https://namache.github.io/BioAce-Privacy-Policy/).
