# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 30, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Ace Education** ("we", "us", or "our") accesses, collects, stores,
uses, and shares your personal information when you use **BioAce**, our mobile application that
teaches biology through a game-style roadmap, practice activities, and achievements (the "Services").

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Services. If you have questions, contact
us at **ace.education.technologies@gmail.com**.

---

## Summary

- **What we collect:** your name and email (via Google Sign-In), the username and avatar you pick,
  your learning progress and gamification data (lessons, XP, streaks, achievements), your friend
  connections, any feedback you submit, and crash/diagnostic data.
- **No ads, no ad tracking:** BioAce shows **no advertising**, contains no ad SDK, and does **not**
  access your device's advertising identifier.
- **No usage analytics:** we do **not** use Google Analytics or any other product-analytics SDK.
- **Sensitive data:** we do **not** collect sensitive personal information (e.g. race, religion,
  sexual orientation, health, precise location) and we do **not** access your contacts, photos,
  files, or microphone.
- **We do not sell your personal information** and we do not share it for advertising.
- **Children:** BioAce is intended for users **aged 13 and older**. We do not knowingly collect data
  from children under 13.
- **Your control:** you can edit your profile, hide yourself from the leaderboard, and
  **permanently delete your account and all associated data from within the app**
  (Settings → Privacy → Delete account).

## 1. Information we collect

### Information you provide

- **Account information** — when you sign in with Google, we receive your **email address**,
  **name**, and **Google account ID** from Google. You also choose a **username** and an in-app
  **avatar** (a picture we supply — BioAce never asks for a photo from your device).
- **Feedback & support** — if you use the in-app feedback form, we collect the **name, device
  description, email, and message** you submit.

### Information created as you use the app

- **Learning & gamification data** — lessons read, quiz/practice results, streaks, XP, ranks,
  achievements, weekly quests, treasure-chest claims, and unlocked avatars.
- **Social data** — the friend requests and friend connections you create, and the
  username/avatar/XP/rank shown on the leaderboard and on your public profile. You can turn
  leaderboard visibility off at any time in **Settings → Privacy**.

### Information collected automatically

- **Crash & diagnostic data** — via Firebase Crashlytics: crash and error reports, device model
  and manufacturer, operating-system version, app version, and a Crashlytics installation
  identifier used to group one device's crash reports together.

### Information stored only on your device

Your app settings (theme, font size, sound, vibration, reminder preference), the cached "Discover"
articles, and which friend requests you have already been notified about are stored **locally on
your device** and are never uploaded to us. The daily streak reminder is a local notification
scheduled on your device.

We do **not** collect information about you from third parties, and we do **not** collect sensitive
personal information.

## 2. How we use your information

- Provide, operate, and maintain the Services (your account, progress, and friend features).
- Save your progress and sync it across devices.
- Show the leaderboard and public profiles, subject to your visibility setting.
- Detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and bugs.
- Respond to your feedback and support requests.
- Maintain security and prevent fraud or abuse.
- Comply with legal obligations.

We do **not** use your information for advertising, ad personalization, or profiling. We process
your information only when we have a valid legal reason to do so.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on: **performance of a contract** (to run your account
and the Services), **legitimate interests** (to keep the app secure, stable, and working — including

## 4. Third-party services we share data with

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising. We share limited
data with the service providers below, who process it on our behalf under their own terms:

| Provider | Purpose | What it may process |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Sign-In** | Authentication | Email, name, Google account ID |
| **Supabase** | Backend, database & hosting of your account and progress | Account, profile, progress, friend, and feedback data |
| **Firebase Crashlytics** | Crash & stability reporting | Crash logs, device & diagnostic data, Crashlytics installation ID |
| **Resend** | Delivering your feedback emails to our team | The name, email, device, and message you submit |

Each provider handles data under its own privacy terms. For Google services, see the
[Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy) page. We may also disclose information
if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

**Public science news.** The "Discover" section fetches public RSS feeds directly from
**phys.org**, **The Guardian**, **ScienceDaily**, **Science News**, and **BBC Science**. We send
those outlets no personal information, but — as with opening any web page — your device's **IP
address** is visible to them, and their own privacy policies apply to that request.

**Server logs.** Our backend providers record standard technical logs (including IP address) to
operate and secure the service. We do not use these logs to build a profile of you.

## 5. Advertising

**BioAce contains no advertising.** There is no ad SDK in the app, we do not access your device's
advertising identifier, and we do not share any data for advertising or cross-context behavioral
advertising purposes.

## 6. Crash reporting

We use **Firebase Crashlytics** to find and fix crashes. Crash reports contain diagnostic data —
device model, OS and app version, and the technical stack trace at the moment of the crash. They do
**not** contain the contents of your lessons, your feedback messages, or your account credentials.
We do not use Google Analytics or any other product-analytics SDK.

## 7. Children's privacy

BioAce is intended for learners **aged 13 and older**, and the app asks you to confirm you are 13 or
older before use. We do **not** knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you
believe a child under 13 has provided us personal information, contact us at
**ace.education.technologies@gmail.com** and we will delete it.

## 8. How long we keep your information

We keep your personal information only as long as needed to provide the Services and for the purposes
described here, unless a longer period is required by law. When you delete your account, your profile
and associated data are removed (see Section 10).

## 9. How we keep your information safe

We use reasonable organizational and technical measures to protect your information:

- All network traffic uses **encrypted HTTPS** connections; plain HTTP is blocked app-wide.
- Your backend data is protected by **row-level security**, so one account cannot read another's
  private data.
- Your sign-in session is stored **encrypted on your device**, under a key held in the Android
  Keystore.
- Device backups of app data are disabled, so your session cannot be extracted from a backup.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
